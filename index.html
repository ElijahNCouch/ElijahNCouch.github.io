概要About

I build software close to the metal — mostly in Rust.

My work spans two studios: Ramp Stack, a cross-platform Rust application and graphics stack, and Space Soup VR, a WGPU-powered 3D engine that runs on the Meta Quest. I care most about the layer where design meets systems — clean abstractions, real-time rendering, and tools that other people build on. I also work across the stack in React, Python, and Java when a product needs it.
